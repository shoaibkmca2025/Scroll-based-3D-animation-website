Most competitors stop at a visitor log the guard types in. Here the pass is scanned — and the entry log and staff attendance fall out of it.

Separation of powers

Built for how committees actually work.

The treasurer cannot touch the gate. A committee member cannot touch the books. The secretary is the only office that can appoint others.

Office The books Operations Gate & staff Appoint others
Secretary Full Full Full Only office that can
Chairman Oversight Oversight Oversight No
Treasurer Maintenance, expenses, funds No No No
Committee member No Amenities, parking, complaints, events Gate staff No

Chairman, secretary and treasurer are single seats — appointing a new one steps the sitting holder down to resident, and the change applies immediately.
